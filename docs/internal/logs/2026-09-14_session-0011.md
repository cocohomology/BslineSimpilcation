# Session 0011 — Review of Local Normal-Branch Feasibility Experiment

Date: 2026-09-14

## Objective

Review Codex-generated feasibility experiment feedback. Treat numerical results as evidence about the certificate, not as proof of the overall theory.

## Main assessment

The experiment supports keeping the local normal-branch certificate as an optional fast verification layer for ordinary close cubic pairs.

Reported behavior:

- ordinary close cases: strong certification success;
- near-self-approach cases: failures concentrated in the expected competing-branch regime.

This confirms the current theoretical interpretation:

- local correspondence is practical in the intended simplification regime;
- global nearest-point/Hausdorff theory is a separate problem and should not be forced into the fast path.

## What the experiment does not validate

It does not prove:

- that the spline simplification problem is solved;
- that candidate generation in q=C'' space is solved;
- that the certificate is universally complete;
- that the approach scales to industrial CAD datasets.

## Important observations

1. The certificate is local. Some certified normal feet are not global nearest points in self-approach cases. This is a boundary of the model, not an immediate failure.

2. The Newton predictor did not show dominant practical advantage. Avoid spending further effort optimizing predictors before understanding the certification geometry itself.

3. Endpoint handling is structural. Any future theorem or implementation must distinguish clamped endpoint cases from strict interior sign conditions.

## Research direction change

The normal certificate has passed its feasibility gate. The main research question returns to the original problem:

\[
q=C''\rightarrow \widetilde q
\rightarrow \widetilde C.
\]

Future work should focus on:

- PL simplification in the second derivative domain;
- breakpoint/jump/kink reduction;
- connection with Lyche-Mørken and free-knot approximation;
- using the existing verification layer to judge candidates.
