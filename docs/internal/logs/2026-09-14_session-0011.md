## Initial assessment

The experiment reports strong success for ordinary close cubic pairs and failures concentrated in near-self-approach cases. This supports continuing investigation of the local certificate as an optional fast path.

However, the experiment does not validate:

- the completeness of the certification architecture;
- the final Hausdorff approximation algorithm;
- candidate generation in q=C'' space;
- scalability to industrial CAD data.

## Important observations

1. The certificate is local. Some certified normal feet are not global nearest points in self-approach cases. This confirms the intended boundary.
2. The Newton predictor did not show the expected dominant advantage. Do not continue optimizing predictors before understanding the certification geometry.
3. The endpoint handling is structural. Public results must distinguish strict interior sign conditions from the clamped endpoint special case.

## Next research question

Before strengthening theory, inspect whether the current certificate is mathematically the right abstraction for simplification, especially for multiple-span candidates and q-space approximation.
