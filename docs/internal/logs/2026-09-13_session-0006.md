# Session 0006 — Algebraic Certification of the Geometry-Aware Bound

Date: 2026-09-13

## Objective

Study one narrow question only:

> After introducing the arc-length tangential quotient in Session 0005, do the normal error, curvature term, and monotonicity condition remain certifiable by fixed-degree one-variable algebra on cubic spline spans?

Do not design the simplification algorithm yet.

## Main result

Yes.

On a union-partition span define
\[
v=C',\quad a=C'',\quad S=v\cdot v,\quad A=E\cdot E,\quad B=E\cdot v,\quad U=E'\cdot E'.
\]
Then
\[
\deg S\le4,\quad \deg A\le6,\quad \deg B\le5,\quad \deg U\le4.
\]

The geometry-aware quantities reduce to fixed-degree rational functions:

- normal error
  \[
  \|P_NE\|^2=\frac{AS-B^2}{S};
  \]
- tangential error
  \[
  |E\cdot T|^2=\frac{B^2}{S};
  \]
- arc-length derivative
  \[
  \|D_sE\|^2=\frac{U}{S};
  \]
- curvature
  \[
  \kappa^2=\frac{W}{S^3},
  \qquad
  W=S(a\cdot a)-(v\cdot a)^2,
  \]
  with the nontrivial degree reduction \(\deg W\le4\).

Exact maxima therefore reduce spanwise to polynomial stationary equations of bounded degree. In particular:

- \(N_{G,\perp}\): degree at most 13;
- \(N_{G,\parallel}\): degree at most 8 after removing the irrelevant zero factor;
- curvature: degree at most 7;
- \(\|D_sE\|\): degree at most 6.

For tolerance decisions, lower-degree polynomial inequalities are often enough.

## Stronger result: exact monotonicity test

Session 0005 used the conservative sufficient condition
\[
\|D_sE\|_\infty+K\|E\|_\infty<1.
\]

This session found a direct test. With
\[
\delta=-E\cdot T=-\frac{B}{\sqrt S},
\qquad
\rho(s)=s+\delta(s),
\]
we obtain
\[
\rho'(s)=\frac{H}{2S^2},
\]
where
\[
\boxed{
H=2S^2-2B'S+BS'.
}
\]
Thus, under regularity \(S>0\),
\[
\boxed{
\rho'>0\iff H>0.
}
\]
The polynomial \(H\) has degree at most eight.

This removes a significant source of conservatism: monotonicity no longer requires combining separate global worst-case bounds.

## Additional structural result

Regularity itself is fixed-degree certifiable because \(S=\|C'\|^2\) is quartic and \(S'\) is cubic.

The proof used reference arc length conceptually, but implementation/certification need not compute arc length or its inverse. All tests are expressed in the native spline parameter through \(E,C',C''\).

## Consequence

The Session 0005 Hausdorff bound
\[
d_H\le N_{G,\perp}+\frac K2N_{G,\parallel}^2
\]
can be certified by independent fixed-degree univariate subproblems plus a polynomial positivity test for the correspondence.

Hence adding first-order parameterization correction does **not** force the problem back into a two-parameter nearest-point/Hausdorff search.

For \(n\) union spans the structure remains linear in \(n\), modulo fixed-degree root isolation and precision refinement.
