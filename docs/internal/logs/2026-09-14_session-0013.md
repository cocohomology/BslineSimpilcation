# Session 0013 — Green metric vs classical approximation

## Goal

Assess whether the Green-induced metric

\[
\|q-\tilde q\|_G=\|G_D(q-\tilde q)\|_\infty
\]

is merely a reformulation of classical spline approximation or can change the structure of the simplification problem.

## Findings

- The metric naturally follows from the geometric reconstruction operator \(D^{-2}\).
- It suppresses high-frequency oscillatory components of \(q=C''\), while remaining sensitive to low-frequency drift.
- This is qualitatively different from coefficient/control-point based error measures.
- However, no evidence yet shows that the induced optimization produces better knot reduction.

## Important caution

The current observation is only a hypothesis. It must be compared against Lyche-Morken style knot removal and existing weak-norm approximation theory.

## Next question

For a fixed approximation space \(V\subset PL\), compare:

\[
\min_{\tilde q\in V}\|q-\tilde q\|_G
\]

with classical projections. Determine whether the optimizer has genuinely different structure.

Do not proceed to algorithm design before this distinction is understood.
